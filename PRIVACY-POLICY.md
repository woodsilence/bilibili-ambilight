# Privacy Policy

- This extension only runs on tabs that start with the URL `https://www.bilibili.com`. The extension will only activate the ambient light effect on Bilibili video pages (`/video/`, `/bangumi/play/`, etc.).
- No personal data is collected or transmitted. All settings and configurations are stored purely locally inside your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- No external tracking, advertising, or profiling requests are sent to any third-party server.
